Where do the fees go?

These are all very approximate:

   | Cost per player       |
---|-----------------------|--
1  | Uniforms              | $25
2  | Player pictures       | $30
3  | Little League fees    | $3
4  | insurance             | $13
5  | software              | 

Other costs are easier to allocate per team:

   | Cost per team                      |
---|------------------------------------|--
5  | baseballs or softballs with bucket | $110
6  | catcher's kit                      | $275
7  | 2 helmets                          | $80

_Every_ game costs us, too:

   | Cost per game                      |
---|------------------------------------|--
8  | 3x game balls                      | $20
9  | youth umpires                      | $20-30 ea
10 | field chalk
11 | field water

Some costs are regular during the season or annual:

   | Regular costs                      |
---|------------------------------------|--
12 | trash service                      |
13 | field repairs                      |
14 | building maintenance               |
15 | consumables


We're partnering with our local schools now and working to upgrade infields
at the local elementary and junior highs:
- bases
- home plates
- pitching mounds
- dugouts
